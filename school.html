    UNDERSTANDING SCHOOL SUPPORT IN THE U.S.

    Two different frameworks for access.

    General overview of IEPs and Section 504 plans in U.S. public schools
    At a glance IEP Section 504 plan
    Purpose Special education and related services for an eligible student. Equal access for an eligible student with a disability.
    Framework Individuals with Disabilities Education Act (IDEA). Section 504 of the Rehabilitation Act.
    What it can include Individualized goals, specially designed instruction, accommodations, and services. Accommodations, related aids, and services needed for equal access.
    Getting started Ask the school about an evaluation and the IEP process. Ask the school’s Section 504 coordinator about evaluation and support.

    Eligibility is individualized. A diagnosis does not automatically establish eligibility, and the setting is not determined by the plan’s name. Ask your school about applicable procedures.

    CONNECTION WITHOUT ASSUMPTIONS

    Make communication a shared responsibility.

    Say what you mean.

    Unspoken rules, sarcasm, and indirect requests are not equally clear to everyone. Explain expectations directly and offer time to respond. Eye contact is not a reliable measure of listening.

    Make space to reset.

    A noisy cafeteria, bright classroom, or sudden change can feel overwhelming. Offer predictable routines and a quieter place to regroup without treating a break as a punishment.
